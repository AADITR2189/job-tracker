What we heard

The brief

An offline-first app for a naval stability engineering team to track jobs, scopes, day-wise planning, team availability and reports. The mark should feel steady, precise and organised, and replace a generic stock anchor icon.

steadypreciseorganisedtrustworthy

Success: ties planning to ships, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, keeps the app's deep olive green.

Concept A

Planning Hull

Four schedule bars, shortest at the bottom, form the side view of a ship: the planning board is the hull.

  • Joins the two things the app is about: planning and ships
  • Built from one shape (a rounded bar) on a 16-unit grid
  • A heavier three-bar cut keeps it clear at 16 px
  • Deep olive green stands apart from blue productivity apps
